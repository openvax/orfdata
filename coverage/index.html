    Released data and gaps¶

    ORFdata keeps datasets in separate species/reference contexts and exposes them through the same discovery, download and query API. This table describes actual data coverage, independently of what the API can represent.

    Species RNA abundance Protein abundance RNA/protein pairing
    Human Wang: 29 specimens, gene/transcript FPKM and estimated copies/cell Wang: 15,210 groups, iBAQ and estimated copies/cell; Pfizer: 10,352 groups, 37 channels, iBAQ/rIBAQ Wang: adjacent cryosections; Pfizer: native RNA missing
    Mouse Pfizer: 606 libraries, 56,051 gene targets, counts Pfizer: 12,124 groups, 63 channels, iBAQ/rIBAQ Unresolved library-to-pool correspondence
    Dog Pfizer: 434 libraries, 30,941 gene targets, counts Pfizer: 9,929 groups, 38 channels, iBAQ/rIBAQ Unresolved library-to-pool correspondence
    Budding yeast CoPY: four RNA libraries plus a source aggregate, FPKM and estimated copies/cell; Ingolia: four RNA libraries, counts and densities CoPY: calibrated SRM and separately calibrated label-free estimates CoPY: explicit source-cohort pairing; individual cross-assay replicate matches unknown

    Peptides and MHC ligands¶

    Dog has three peptide-level datasets:

    • Pfizer native peptide evidence (guide): 953,980 target peptide sequences, native peptide/PSM reporter quantities and reported protein-group memberships. The 96 runs retain 38 tissue pools, four bridge channels and two empty channels; source memberships and searched-FASTA candidates stay separate.
    • Canine PeptideAtlas (guide, build 573): 149,546 tryptic peptides with PSM counts in 18 Labrador tissue, plasma and urine samples, every protein location, and 17,795 protein calls, 17,110 of them mapped to Ensembl 110 proteins with an identical sequence. PeptideAtlas states no licence, so this dataset is built locally from its download and never redistributed.
    • DLA class I ligands (guide, Kaabinejadian et al.): 76,550 PEAKS rows from four dog tumours, each searched against four protein databases, and from three human HCT 116 lines that each express one DLA-88 allele.

    All three searched known proteins only. Other species have no peptide-level measurement dataset yet; the human and mouse ORF catalogues carry pooled peptide evidence for their noncanonical ORFs.

    Reading the table¶

    • Gene targets include measured zeros; their presence in a matrix does not imply expression in every sample.
    • Protein-group representatives do not establish unique protein attribution.
    • TMT channels are not independent donors.
    • Wang processing views do not add specimens, and gene-level comparisons do not resolve individual protein isoforms or noncanonical ORFs.

    Calibration and uncertainty remain attached to observations:

    • CoPY RNA copies/cell assume 60,000 total mRNAs. Its 1,903 SRM summaries contain 1,167 point estimates, 668 upper bounds and 68 unquantifiable results.
    • Human Wang protein copies use the proteomic-ruler estimate; RNA copies assume mRNA is 2% of total RNA mass. These calibrations remain distinct from yeast isotope-standard measurements.

    ORF evidence and ribosome profiling¶

    The separate existence-evidence catalogue contains 7,264 human noncanonical ORFs, including 2,186 with reported MS support. Mouse has 60,707 ORFs: Ribo-seq ORFs from Chang et al. and Yang et al., and 2,976 OpenProt 2.2 ORFs with at least two unique MS peptides, built from their sources' files. Yang et al.'s Ribo-seq ORFs cover zebrafish (15,491), C. elegans (4,035) and yeast (1,369), and with Chang et al.'s are optional human catalogues; dog has no experimental ncORF catalogue, because no dog Ribo-seq or ncORF MS search is public (details). Its ribosome evidence and pooled PSM counts are not sample-level ribosome occupancy or protein abundance.

    The separate Ingolia yeast release contains four Ribo-seq libraries across 13,748 source regions, alongside four RNA libraries. Exact region coordinates and RNA/RPF aliquot pairing remain unresolved. Other species do not yet have released quantitative Ribo-seq datasets in this registry.

    More detail¶

    See the short guides for matched human tissues, protein measurements, native dog/mouse RNA, yeast RNA/protein calibration, yeast RNA/ribosome occupancy and complete search sequences. The roadmap tracks further human comparators, quantitative Ribo-seq, ncORF evidence and additional species. Cat, cattle, rat and other taxa use the same model when source datasets are added. Orthology comparisons are explicit and never transfer observations or protein-existence evidence between species.
